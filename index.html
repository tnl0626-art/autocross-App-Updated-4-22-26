Damping — Front
Front CompressioniControls how fast the front suspension compresses under braking and turn-in. More compression = sharper transient response and less dive. Too firm = harshness over bumps, reduced front grip. —
—
Front ReboundiControls how fast the front suspension extends after compression. More rebound = tire stays planted after hitting a bump. Too much = tire hop and loss of grip over successive bumps. —
—
Damping — Rear
Rear CompressioniControls how fast the rear suspension compresses under acceleration and mid-corner load. More = better body control exiting corners. Too firm = rear skips over bumps, loses traction. —
—
Rear ReboundiControls how fast the rear extends after compression. More rebound = rear stays planted on corner exit. Too much = inside rear lifts under load transfer, reducing traction. —
